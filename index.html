A little about me

Curious by default.
Practical by practice.

Throughout my career, I have learned how much it matters to stay focused on users. Whether it is digital marketing or making content understandable for different audiences, thinking about what users need has become central to my work. Localizing content taught me how to understand users and keep language clear.

I work across UX writing and content design, content localization, multilingual product content, UX research support, and content strategy. I am a native Japanese and English speaker, with professional Spanish and intermediate German and Portuguese.
