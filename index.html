02 · Nike By You

Making sneaker customization feel personal

In a Bitesize UX workshop, I rewrote four moments in the sneaker customization flow. I used a celebratory tone when people finished a design and a more direct tone when they had a choice to make. For example, I changed “You’re a rockstar Designer!” to “Want to add some text?” so people could tell what they were being asked to decide before checkout.

About

From Localization to UX

I’m Ayano. I started in localization, adapting digital products for different languages and markets. That work taught me to spot where an unclear label or a missing bit of context could leave someone unsure what to do next. Moving into UX and content design gave me a chance to tackle those problems earlier, while the experience was still taking shape.

Today I design user flows, write interface copy in Japanese and English, and create style guides that help teams keep language consistent. I use AI to explore early options, then review and refine them based on what users need at each step.
